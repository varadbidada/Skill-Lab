# ✨ AI Toolkit 2026

### Less busywork. More creativity. Smarter building.

Welcome to **AI Toolkit 2026** --- a student-friendly guide to AI tools
that can help you study, research, organise your work, design
interfaces, and build web applications.

The goal isn't to try every tool or collect bookmarks. It's to **find
the right tool for a real task, experiment with it, and check the
result**.

> 💡 **Workshop mindset:** Pick a problem → choose a tool → try it →
> inspect the output → improve it.

## 📚 Research & productivity

### [Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM)](https://notebooklm.google.com/)

Turn your own source material into a more useful study resource. Add
supported documents and sources, then ask questions, create summaries,
and explore the material with answers grounded in those sources.

**Try it for:** Turning lecture PDFs into revision notes or asking
questions about your own study material.

### [Emily --- find the relevant service](https://www.google.com/search?q=Emily+AI+email+management+assistant)

The workshop list describes Emily as an AI assistant for email
management, drafting personalised responses, and helping streamline team
communication.

**Try it for:** Exploring how an AI assistant might help draft or
organise email.\
*Note: "Emily" can refer to multiple products, so confirm the exact
service before signing up.*

### [Mintify --- find the relevant service](https://www.google.com/search?q=Mintify+AI+budgeting+financial+management+tool)

The workshop list describes Mintify as a financial-management tool for
tracking spending, budgeting, invoicing, and visualising expenses.

**Try it for:** Exploring how a budgeting workflow could help organise
student expenses.\
*Note: Several products use the Mintify name; verify that the result
matches the tool you intend to use.*

### [Glasp](https://glasp.co/)

Highlight and annotate online reading, collect useful ideas, and
organise insights from articles, videos, and other web content.

**Try it for:** Collecting useful sources and notes while researching a
project.

## 💡 Visual thinking

### [Napkin AI](https://www.napkin.ai/)

Transform text into diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, and other visuals
that help explain ideas. You can refine the visuals and export them for
use in notes or presentations.

**Try it for:** Turning a networking chapter or project workflow into a
visual concept map.

## 🌍 Creative worlds

### [Skybox AI by Blockade Labs](https://skybox.blockadelabs.com/)

Explore the creation of panoramic 360° environments from text prompts.

**Try it for:** Imagining a futuristic campus, game environment, or
creative virtual world.

## 🎨 UI/UX & prototyping

### [Figma AI / Figma Make](https://www.figma.com/ai/)

Explore AI-assisted design workflows, generate or refine interface
concepts, and turn ideas into prototypes that can be reviewed and
iterated on.

**Try it for:** Sketching the first version of a student app interface.

### [Motiff](https://www.motiff.com/)

Motiff was an AI-assisted UI design tool for generating and refining
interfaces and maintaining consistent layouts.

⚠️ **Availability note:** Motiff's official site currently states that
the product has been discontinued and that data export is available
until **31 October 2026**. Treat it as a reference or existing-project
export option rather than a tool to start a new project with.

### [Visily](https://www.visily.ai/)

Create editable UI mockups from text prompts, screenshots, diagrams, or
other visual references. It is designed to help people communicate
product ideas without needing to be expert designers.

**Try it for:** Turning a rough sketch of a college app into an editable
mockup.

### [Google Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/)

Explore interface concepts from natural-language descriptions and
user-flow ideas, then refine the generated design.

**Try it for:** Generating a first-pass dashboard or mobile-screen
concept from a prompt.

### [Moonchild AI](https://moonchild.ai/)

Generate and refine product screens and multi-screen flows, with a focus
on keeping designs aligned with a design system.

**Try it for:** Exploring several connected screens for a
task-management app.

### [Uizard](https://uizard.io/)

Turn ideas, text descriptions, or sketches into UI concepts and
interactive prototypes that can help communicate an app idea.

**Try it for:** Converting a hand-drawn login and dashboard sketch into
a prototype.

## 💻 Coding & app building

### [v0 by Vercel](https://v0.app/)

Generate and refine user interfaces and web applications from
natural-language prompts. It can help produce code and connect the
design process to a development workflow.

**Try it for:** Creating a first draft of a responsive portfolio or
dashboard.

### [Lovable](https://lovable.dev/)

Build and iterate on web-app prototypes by describing what you want in
natural language. Review the generated application and keep refining it
to match your requirements.

**Try it for:** Prototyping a club-event registration page or simple
task tracker.

### [Bolt.new](https://bolt.new/)

Create and iterate on web applications in the browser with an
AI-assisted development workflow.

**Try it for:** Quickly exploring a working prototype of a student
project idea.

## 🗺️ Website planning & UX

### [Relume](https://www.relume.io/)

Help plan a website before building it by exploring sitemaps, user
flows, and wireframes. Its workflows can connect with tools such as
Figma and Webflow.

**Try it for:** Planning the pages and navigation for a college club
website.

### [UX Pilot](https://uxpilot.ai/)

Explore UX/UI concepts, wireframes, and product flows from a sketch,
prompt, or product brief. It can help teams visualise and refine an
interface before development.

**Try it for:** Creating a first wireframe for a student-focused mobile
app.

## 🧪 Mini-challenge: pick a problem

Choose one task and find a tool that fits:

-   **Study smarter:** Turn your lecture notes into a revision resource.
-   **Explain visually:** Turn a complicated concept into a flowchart.
-   **Design an app:** Turn a rough idea into a UI mockup.
-   **Build a website:** Generate a frontend prototype from a clear
    prompt.
-   **Plan a project:** Map out the pages and user journey before
    coding.

Be ready to explain **why you chose the tool, what you asked it to do,
and what you changed after reviewing the result**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🛡️ Use AI thoughtfully

-   **Verify important information.** AI-generated content can be
    incomplete or wrong.
-   **Review generated code.** Run it, test it, and understand the
    changes before relying on it.
-   **Protect your data.** Never paste passwords, API keys, private
    records, or confidential information into tools.
-   **Check access and pricing.** Features, free tiers, and account
    requirements can change.
-   **Respect ownership.** Check usage terms before publishing generated
    content or using brand assets.

Some tools may have limited access, change names, or be discontinued.
The links above are intended as starting points; check the official site
for current availability.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🚀 The takeaway

**Don't collect AI tools. Learn to solve problems with them.**

Pick one tool. Try one real task. Check the output. Improve it. Then
share what you learned.
