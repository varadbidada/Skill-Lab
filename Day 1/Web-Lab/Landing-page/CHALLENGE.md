# Landing Page Sprint

Build a one-screen pitch for a real or fictional product, student club, event, or app.

## Required
- Clear headline and short explanation
- One obvious call-to-action
- Intentional typography, spacing, and contrast
- Responsive layout on narrow and wide screens
- Content that is yours, not untouched placeholder copy

## Choose your level
- Core: customize copy, colors, and layout.
- Stretch: add a working theme toggle or a feature/pricing section.
- Boss: add an interaction with keyboard support and a reduced-motion-friendly animation.

You have 35 minutes. You are judged on clarity, craft, responsiveness, and how well you explain your decisions—not how closely you copy a reference.
